[center][size=6][b]Dragonkin Plus[/b][/size]
[size=3]Multipliers, cheats and quality-of-life — all in one in-game panel[/size]
[size=3][color=#ff9900][b]Requires the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/dragonkinthebanished/mods/11]Dragonkin Mod Loader[/url][/b][/color][/size][/center]

[size=4][b]What is it?[/b][/size]
Dragonkin Plus adds five tabs to the Mod Loader's F7 panel. Everything is changed with sliders and checkboxes [b]while you play[/b], and every change is undone the moment you switch the mod off.

[size=4][b]Multipliers[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]XP, wyrmling XP and [b]city XP[/b]
[*]Gold, equipment drops, equipment and loot quality, materials, eggs, skill fragments, hatch chance
[*]Pickup range
[*]Damage, critical damage, max health, healing
[*]Cooldowns, skill cost, resource gain
[*]Movement speed, attack speed, dodge distance
[/list]

[size=4][b]Cheats[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]God mode
[*]Unlimited mana, potions and dodge
[*]Ultimate always full
[*]Game speed
[*]Unlock all waypoints, open the teleport map anywhere
[*]Attribute points
[/list]

[size=4][b]Convenience[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Camera distance[/b] — zoom out further
[*]Enemy health and damage
[*]Extra movement speed in the city
[*]"I'm stuck!" button and three saved positions per map
[*][b]Bigger bag and bigger chests[/b]
[*][b]Sell junk-marked items anywhere[/b]
[*]Crafting gold cost
[*]Auto potion at low health
[*]Hide the level-up notification
[*]The game's hidden [b]DPS meter[/b]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Console and Status[/b][/size]
Developer switches left in the game (for example: always show enemy health bars), plus a live view of the connection state and every player attribute.

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/dragonkinthebanished/mods/11]Dragonkin Mod Loader[/url] first.
[*]Download this mod and extract it into the game folder [i]Dragonkin - The Banished[/i].
     You should end up with [i]DragonkinTheBanished\Binaries\Win64\Mods\DragonkinPlus\DragonkinPlus.dll[/i].
[*]In game press [b]F7[/b] → [b]Mods[/b] → tick [b]Dragonkin Plus[/b] and [b]Allow native code[/b].
     The five tabs appear immediately.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Switch it off in the Mods tab, then delete [i]Binaries\Win64\Mods\DragonkinPlus[/i].
[color=#ff5555][b]If you enlarged the bag or chests, empty the extra slots first.[/b][/color] Without the mod the game only has its normal number of slots, and items in the extra slots are lost.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Made for [b]offline / solo play[/b]. Online cities and co-op are checked by the server.
[*]A new bag size applies at the next area load, a new chest size at the next game start.
[*]Settings are saved in [i]Mods\DragonkinPlus\DragonkinPlus.ini[/i]; problems are logged in [i]DragonkinPlus.log[/i] — please attach it to bug reports.
[*]7 languages, following the Mod Loader's language setting.
[*]Every file was scanned on VirusTotal: 0 detections.
[*]Uses Dear ImGui (MIT license).
[/list]
